# Changelog

## 0.3.0: Refinement pass
- **Snowball fixed.** Might grows at half speed past 10 cards; prey grows with your body and with depth; long fights frenzy ordinary enemies; Big lands two extra wounds at 18+; growth stops at 30, and skipping a devour pays.
- **See what a card will do.** Selecting a card previews the damage on the enemy's health bar (skull if it kills) and the wounds a guard saves. Slide across the hand to read every card.
- **Read the enemy.** Numbered badges show who acts first; the wound banner names who hit you; patterns appear once you've watched them cycle; poison that will kill before it acts is marked.
- **Choices that matter.** Elite bone choice, scar cap per fight, Heavy blocks a wound each turn, a Burrower before every boss, unique upgrades, wound-trigger and lost-body cards, spending cards for Venom and Storm. All 30 events and 60 bones are in.
- **Learning.** A guided first fight, How to Play from the pause menu, a full glossary, first-sighting tips for every enemy move, a death breakdown and your final deck on the results screen.
- **Feel.** Tear vs peel, hit-all brushstrokes, stippled poison, a cracking block plate, coil rings on the body, a devour gulp, depth-shaded map, a turning ring for the Devour ending.
- **Sound.** Layered tear and swallow, per-act music layers that thin as you empty, a sound for each enemy move, vibration, volume sliders. Recorded takes drop into `audio/`.
- **Phones.** Landscape layout, rotation that settles, installable and playable offline.
- **Access.** Undo assist, left-handed layout, larger hand with larger text, screen-reader labels, gamepad glyphs.
- **Tools.** Error log you can copy, save export/import, replay any seed from history, where-you-die stats.
