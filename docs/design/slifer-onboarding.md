# Slifer / Spend Yourself: Onboarding

Companion to `slifer.md`. Every rule is taught by play, one verb at a time, with as little text as possible (rule 8). Copy below is the maximum; cut any line playtests show is unneeded. Teaching happens on the first run's real act 1 map, not a separate tutorial mode.

## 0. Title to first fight (under 30 seconds)

- The title plate: Uro coiled in the dark, tail almost in its mouth. **Begin** starts a run with Venom; the molt picker appears only after Tide unlocks.
- Map row 1 is a single fight node, already highlighted.

## 1. Fight 1: your hand is your body (one Gnawer)

- Opening hand of 6. The coiled body along the bottom pulses once.
- **Prompt:** "Play Fang" (pointer at a Fang). After the play, the might number drops by 1 and the body visibly shortens. **No text explains it**; the shortening is the lesson.
- The Gnawer's intent shows claw 1. On the enemy turn, the hand lifts and the game waits: **"Choose 1 to lose."** This is the only time the line is explained with a pointer.
- The fight is tuned to end in 3 turns.

## 2. Fight 2: might and coil (a Gnawer and a Tunneler)

- **Prompt (once):** "Bigger hand, bigger hit." Shown the first time the might number changes a card's damage by 2 or more.
- At the end of turn 1, held cards gain their first gold ring with a creak. On turn 2, a card at coil 1 shows its raised number in gold. No text.
- If the player plays every card on turn 1 and reaches 2 or fewer cards, the body frays and the pulse turns urgent. That warning is the lesson about spending too fast.

## 3. Fight 3: devour (Spitter and Carrion Crow)

- The Crow's eat intent (a maw icon) appears. When it eats, the highest-coil card visibly tears out of the body.
- After the fight, the husk screen: two husks float up. **Prompt:** "Devour one." Picking it adds the card and the body grows a segment (+1 max hand).

## 4. First rest: scars

- If the player has scars, the rest screen highlights Mend. **Prompt (once):** "Scars shrink you." Otherwise the first scar later in the run triggers the same one-time line on the results screen.

## 5. First Big moment

- The first time the hand reaches 9 cards, the body thickens and enemy claw numbers tick up by 1 with a thud. **Prompt (once):** "Big: easier to hit."

## 6. Unlock gating

| Unlock | When |
|---|---|
| Molt picker and Tide | Reach act 2 |
| Storm | Defeat an act 3 boss |
| Turns | Win once (either ending) |
| Daily Descent | Complete 3 runs |
| Card library and history | After the first run ends |

## 7. Assists (settings, never shame)

- **Intent detail:** show exact upcoming patterns for 2 turns.
- **Wound confirm:** require a second tap to confirm each wound choice (on by default on touch).
- **Undo last card** (until the turn ends). Disables leaderboard submission for the run.
- **Text size, reduced motion, high-contrast ink, colorblind-safe intent icons** (icons are already shape-distinct).
- **Fight speed:** 1×, 1.5×, 2× enemy animations.
